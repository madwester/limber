### Private Pilates Class Benefits

While our group classes are intentionally kept intimate with plenty of hands-on cues and modifications, semi-private sessions exist in a completely different category.

Here is what makes our semi-private setup unique:

**Custom Programme, Written Just for You:** In group classes, everyone follows a shared flow. In a semi-private, you work off your own tailored routine designed specifically for your body, goals, and season of life – progressed and adapted for every session.

**Pilates Apparatus Access:** You aren't restricted to a single mat. You get to work across the full range of specialised Pilates equipment in our studio that includes the Cadillac, Wunda Chair, and Reformer.

**Granular Biomechanical Progression:** Instead of quick realignments mid-class, your teacher has the time to have a longer chat, break down your exact movement mechanics, address underlying imbalances, and help you tick off specific, complex strength goals.

**100% Paced to Your Body:** Research published by [Healthdirect Australia](https://www.healthdirect.gov.au/healthy-living/physical-activity/health-benefits-of-yoga-and-pilates) highlights how targeted equipment training builds deep joint stability, core strength, and long-term postural health. You can achieve this in a semi-private as you move at your own pace.

**Complete Autonomy for Rehab & Pregnancy:** When managing acute recovery or pre/post-natal care, standard class flows can feel restrictive. As noted by the [Better Health Channel](https://www.betterhealth.vic.gov.au/pilates-health-benefits), personalised sessions provide the structural support needed to rebuild strength safely without any guesswork or risk of overtraining.

### Frequently Asked Questions About Semi-Private Pilates at Limber

**How many people are in a semi-private pilates class?**
Semi-privates are our most focused intimate group experience of up to four people, to ensure every person has all the benefits of personalised attention.

**Do I need prior experience at a pilates studio?**
Not at all. The whole idea is the programme is tailored to your body and needs. We have people who are beginners through to people who have been practicing for years.

**How qualified are the instructors leading semi-private sessions?**
Our teachers are diploma-trained with a minimum of a comprehensive year-long program behind them, years of hands-on experience, and a genuine commitment to ongoing education. You're in excellent hands.
